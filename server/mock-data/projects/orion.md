# Project Orion: brief

> Fictional project for demo purposes.

## Client and goal
Harbor Mutual Insurance wants a new mobile app so policyholders can view policies, file claims with photos and track claim status. The first release is planned for the app stores in about four months.

## Technology
- React Native with TypeScript, Expo for development builds
- Firebase for push notifications and analytics
- Client identity via the client's single sign-on
- Builds distributed through TestFlight and Google Play internal testing

## Team and ways of working
The team is small and works in two-week sprints with a demo to the client every second Friday. An NDA is required before access to client systems. Store publishing rights are restricted by the client.

## What new joiners typically work on
Mobile developers build claim and policy screens, integrate the camera and photo upload, and fix device-specific issues found in the device lab. Responsibilities for other roles on Orion have not been written up yet.
